Three steps to carry all 45 pins in Google Maps

The whole shortlist ships as two files in this project. Import once and the list lives in your own Google account — usable offline in the mountains.

  1. Download the CSV

    Grab maps.csv — all 45 pins with name, area, tier, price, rating and notes, formatted for import.

    Download CSV

  2. Import to My Maps

    On a laptop open google.com/mymaps, then My Maps → Create a new map → Import and upload the CSV (or KML). When it asks “Choose columns to position your placemarks”, tick Address → Continue; then pick Name as the title column. If it offers grouping, choose Group to split pins into stays / hotels / places / cafes.

    Open Google My Maps

  3. Open in Google Maps

    On your phone, open the Google Maps app → Saved → Maps. Your imported Jibhi–Shoja map appears with every pin, ready for Oct 4–8.

    Open Google Maps
